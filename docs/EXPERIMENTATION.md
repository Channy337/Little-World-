# Civoria experimentation model

Civorians are allowed to invent, infer, test, fail, revise, and discover.

The simulation does not use a fixed technology tree. A person may propose a hypothesis only from resources they carry or things already present in their personal knowledge. The proposal is stored on that person's persistent mind.

A proposed experiment has a hypothesis, an operation, a small list of materials, and a hoped-for result. Supported operations are deliberately generic: observe, combine, shape, stack, soak, dry, heat, and compare. They are not technologies or recipes.

The world resolves attempts deterministically from that person's curiosity, relevant accumulated skill, stress, experience, and whether the required materials are actually available to them. Success is not guaranteed. Failed and inconclusive attempts are remembered too.

A successful experiment becomes personal knowledge for the discoverer. It does not become global civilization knowledge automatically. Later systems can spread discoveries through conversation, teaching, imitation, apprenticeships, records, and institutions.

This layer is intentionally a bridge. It creates earned discovery without pretending the current physical engine can already simulate arbitrary machines or architecture. Future structure and production systems should consume discoveries from this layer instead of unlocking from a scripted tech tree.
