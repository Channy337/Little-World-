# Civoria experimentation model

Civorians are allowed to invent, infer, test, fail, revise, and discover.

The simulation does not use a fixed technology tree. A person may propose a hypothesis only from resources they carry or things already present in their personal knowledge. The proposal is stored on that person's persistent mind.

A proposed experiment has a hypothesis, an operation, a small list of materials, and a hoped-for result. Supported operations are deliberately generic: observe, combine, shape, stack, soak, dry, heat, and compare. They are not technologies or recipes.

An idea does not resolve merely because a day passed. The person must spend canonical work time and consume carried materials. The engine evaluates the operation and material signature against Civoria's physical affordances; the AI's wording never determines the result. Failed attempts are remembered too.

A single useful result remains promising evidence. Repeating the same physical result creates personal knowledge and an executable capability for the discoverer. It does not become global civilization knowledge automatically. Later systems can spread discoveries through conversation, teaching, imitation, apprenticeships, records, and institutions.

The first complete affordance is shaping carried wood into timber. A person who proves it twice can deliberately repeat the process, and timber contributes more construction value than raw wood. Buildings record the processed material and method actually used. This narrow vertical slice is the foundation for fire, tools, components, machines, and broader physical rules; it does not pretend the current engine already simulates arbitrary aircraft or chemistry.
