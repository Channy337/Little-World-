What is happening

    About Clivoria

    Clivoria is one persistent shared civilization. The browser is only a window into it. The canonical world lives on the server, advances over real time, and every visitor sees the same villagers, buildings, resources, and history.

    • Needs shape behavior. Hunger, energy, and loneliness rise over time. Villagers react to what is most urgent instead of following a fixed story.
    • Work changes the settlement. Villagers gather resources, farm, mine, trade, build homes, and eventually support a market.
    • Life continues. Relationships can lead to new villagers. People age, families change, and the settlement carries its history forward.
    • The world is shared. Upstash stores one canonical world per environment, while the server and heartbeat advance it even when no browser is open.

    Current Civoria 0.2 villagers use deterministic built-in instincts. Persistent server-side AI minds are a later milestone, so the visual upgrade does not pretend that feature already exists.

    A persistent civilization experiment. One shared world, watched through your browser.
