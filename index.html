    About Clivoria

    Clivoria is one persistent shared civilization beginning from a primitive state. The browser is only a window into it, while the canonical world continues on the server.

    • No inherited civilization. The first people have no homes, professions, farms, market, controlled fire, or recipes.
    • Ideas are not magic. A hypothesis changes nothing until somebody spends time and materials testing it physically.
    • Knowledge is personal. A repeatable result becomes a capability only for the person who proved it.
    • Discover world effects. Processed materials enter inventory and visibly become part of constructed objects.
    • No required history. The world validates consequences rather than forcing Civorians to copy humanity's technology tree.

    A persistent civilization experiment. One shared world, watched through your browser.
